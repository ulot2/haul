# Haul promo: storyboard (v1)

**Format:** 1080×1920, 60 fps, about 42 s. **Tempo:** 120 BPM, so one beat = 0.5 s = 30 frames and one bar = 2 s.
The whole edit sits on that grid: every cut is on a downbeat, and the small moves (ticks, checks, chips) land on beats or eighth notes.

**Look:** Freight dark. Ground #121110, phone and sheets on #1C1B19 / #2A2825, hairlines #36332E, ink #F4F0E8, muted #A8A298.
Clay #C8775A only on what's tapped and what moves (the picked card, buttons, progress, the tile, checks).
Captions: Archivo Condensed Black in capitals, top third, kept clear of the TikTok/Reels UI zones. Manifest labels in JetBrains Mono.
Posts in the feed are abstract: tall tonal cards (the app's six thumb tones) with a mono site tag (TT, IG, X, YT) and a ▶.
No real videos, no platform logos or interfaces.

**Music:** an original track, written as code (a small synth in Python). Warm, minimal house at 120 BPM in D minor
(Dm, B♭, F, C, two bars per chord). Filtered intro, the drop at 0:04, a half-time break at 0:32, a last hit on the logo, then the chord rings out.

## The other cuts (same project, same scenes)
- **1:1 (1080×1080), ~42 s:** the same timeline. The phone sits left of centre and smaller; the captions move to the right column; the type-only scenes and the logo re-centre.
- **Teaser (9:16, 8 s, four bars):** the hook (bar 1) → share + toast (bar 3, cut tighter) → "Saved to Movies/Haul" (bar 6) → logo, line and CTA (bar 19), with its own four-bar mix of the same track.

## How it's built
- `promo/` holds one HTML page per format that draws any frame from a single time value (SVG + CSS, the app's own Archivo and JetBrains Mono files, the logo paths copied from `ic_launcher_fg.xml`).
- Playwright steps through every frame (no real-time playback, so no dropped frames); FFmpeg encodes H.264 + AAC.
- The music and every sound effect are synthesised by a Python script from the same timeline file, so the sound lands on the frame.
